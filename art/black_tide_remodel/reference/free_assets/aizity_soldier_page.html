Description

A modern special-forces soldier in black tactical gear: a helmet with red-tinted goggles, a plate carrier with pouches, a backpack, gloves, knee pads and boots, standing in an A-pose. It is designed as a digital character for games, film and visualisation.

Technical details: about 282,000 polygons and 197,000 vertices, UV-mapped, with 19 materials and high-resolution texture maps, at about 1.8 m tall. The heavy detail is meant for hero shots and close-ups; build LODs before using several in real time.

Available as GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender (.blend). Use the GLB for web viewers and three.js, the FBX for Unity, Unreal or Cinema 4D, the OBJ as a widely supported static mesh, and the .blend to edit it directly in Blender. AiZity cleaned up the original (centred, grounded and renamed the parts) and converted it to these formats.

Ideal for tactical shooter games, military and police film previs, character design references and action posters.
